# Sales Performance Analysis | SQL & Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a U.S Superstore retail sales data from 2015 to 2018 to identify revenue trends, business growth, and sales performance across different product categories and regions.

Using **PostgreSQL, SQL, and Microsoft Power BI**, I analyzed approximately $2.26 million in revenue and 5,000 orders. I developed an interactive dashboard to turn raw sales data into clear, useful business insights.

The goal of this project was to strengthen my data analysis skills and demonstrate how businesses can use data to make informed decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- **PostgreSQL:** Stored and managed the sales dataset.
- **SQL:** Analyzed sales performance using aggregations, CTEs, and window functions.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Created an interactive dashboard with KPIs, charts, and filters.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Worked with the original sales dataset.

## Dashboard Features

The interactive Power BI dashboard includes:

- **Total Revenue:** Tracks overall sales performance.
- **Total Orders:** Measures the number of orders placed.
- **Average Order Value:** Shows average revenue per order.
- **Annual Revenue:** Compares sales performance across years.
- **Monthly Revenue Trends:** Visualizes revenue changes over time.
- **Revenue by Category:** Compares Furniture, Technology, and Office Supplies.
- **Regional Revenue:** Identifies sales performance across regions.
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** Measures annual revenue growth.
- **Year Slicer:** Allows users to filter dashboard results by year.

## SQL Analysis

I used SQL to answer business questions such as:

1. How much revenue did the business generate each year?
2. How did revenue change from one year to the next?
3. Which product categories generated the most revenue?
4. Which regions contributed the most sales?
5. How did monthly revenue change over time?

**SQL techniques used:**
- `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, and `ORDER BY`
- Aggregate functions such as `SUM()`, `COUNT()`, and `AVG()`
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window functions such as `LAG()`
- Date extraction and calculations
- Year-over-year growth calculations

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Growth

Revenue declined by approximately 4.26% in 2016 before recovering with 30.64% growth in 2017 and 20.30% growth in 2018.

This suggests that the business experienced a temporary decline followed by two years of strong revenue growth.

### 2. Product Category Performance

The dashboard compares revenue across three product categories: Furniture, Technology, and Office Supplies.

This analysis helps identify which categories contribute most to total sales and where businesses may want to focus their sales strategies.

### 3. Regional Sales Performance

Analyzing revenue by region helps identify stronger and weaker sales markets.

These findings can support decisions about regional marketing, sales strategies, and resource allocation.

### 4. Monthly Sales Trends

The monthly revenue chart helps identify periods of higher and lower sales activity.

Businesses can use this information to investigate seasonal patterns and improve sales planning.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business could:

- Investigate the factors behind the 2016 revenue decline.
- Identify strategies that contributed to the strong growth in 2017 and 2018.
- Focus marketing efforts on promising product categories and regions.
- Use monthly revenue trends to improve sales forecasting and planning.
